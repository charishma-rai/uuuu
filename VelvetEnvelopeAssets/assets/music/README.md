# Music & Audio Specifications

This directory holds atmospheric background music loops and sound effect placeholders for Velvet Envelope.

## Required Track Placeholders
- `intro.mp3`: Main menu theme. Dark, mysterious orchestral composition with cello solos and light piano accents. Recommended length: 2:00 - 3:00 (seamless loop).
- `investigation.mp3`: Crime scene & evidence search track. Subtle, low-tempo noir jazz or moody ambient strings. Recommended length: 3:00 - 5:00 (seamless loop).
- `success.mp3`: Case solved stinger / victory music. Triumphant orchestral crescendo. Recommended length: 0:15 - 0:30.
- `failure.mp3`: Wrong accusation / case cold stinger. Somber brass and descending piano chord. Recommended length: 0:15 - 0:30.

## Audio Specifications
- **Format**: MP3 (192kbps or 320kbps) or OGG Vorbis
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1kHz stereo
